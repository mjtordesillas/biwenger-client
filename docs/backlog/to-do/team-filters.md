Filter/sort controls for the my-team screen, tucked behind an expandable
panel opened by a "filters" button (currently the position filter is
always visible; it moves in under this control).

- Filter by position (existing).
- Sort by: position, or market value — each ascending/descending.
- Default: current ordering (unsorted/unfiltered), i.e. opening the app
  shows the team exactly as today unless filters/sort are applied.
